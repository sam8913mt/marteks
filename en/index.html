Why Merteks?

A small team means fewer middlemen, faster answers and clearer responsibility.

  • Straight to the builders

    The same two people who talk to you design and code the project. Nobody sits in between.

  • Design before code

    Look and layout are defined and reviewed with you before development starts, so there are fewer surprises.

  • Clear time and cost

    The consultation and first estimate are free, and time and cost are agreed before work begins.

  • Tested on phone and desktop

    Every page is checked on different screens before delivery so it stays tidy and fast everywhere.
